## Notes

- **pygame-ce (LGPL 2.1).** pygame-ce is used as an unmodified, separately installed
  library. When Air Rhythm is distributed as a packaged build, the pygame-ce library files
  must remain replaceable by the user (PyInstaller's one-folder mode does this), and this
  notice plus the LGPL text must be included.
- **MediaPipe Hand Landmarker model.** Google's model card ("MediaPipe Hands (Lite/Full)",
  Oct 2021) states it is licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0, and lists hand
  control and entertainment among its intended uses. The game downloads it from
  `storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/hand_landmarker/hand_landmarker/float16/1/`
  and checks its SHA-256 before use.
- **OpenCV.** The `opencv-python` wheels also bundle third-party libraries (e.g. FFmpeg
  under LGPL); their notices ship inside the package as `cv2/LICENSE-3RD-PARTY.txt`.
- **Apache 2.0 components** require keeping their license text and NOTICE files when
  redistributing them. These are included inside each installed package
  (`*.dist-info/licenses/`) and must be kept in packaged builds.

## Music and sounds

All music, sound effects and beatmaps in Air Rhythm are generated by the game's own code
(`audio_gen.py`, `map_gen.py`) and are covered by the MIT License. No third-party audio
samples are used.
